## The Architecture: 430 Wireframes Decoded

### User Trinity
```
🟠 Supervisors (Parents/Coaches)
    ├── Pay subscriptions
    ├── Manage up to 6 Players
    └── Approve expenditures
    
🟢 Players (Students)  
    ├── Build debate identity
    ├── Join teams & activities
    └── Earn achievements
    
🟣 Enablers (Judges/Admins)
    ├── Require certification
    ├── Generate ballots
    └── Earn emCoins
```

- **6 Platforms in 1**: Education + Social + Payment + Gaming + Content + Events

- **Trust Architecture**: Grey states, verification flows, approval chains—all serving child safety
