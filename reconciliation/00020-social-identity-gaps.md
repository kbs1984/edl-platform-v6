# Social Identity Gaps Analysis

**Session**: 00020  
**Date**: 2025-08-17  
**Focus**: Team and community building gaps for educational identity

---

## Summary

The system has basic team infrastructure (3 tables) but lacks all social identity features that would make students feel connected and part of a learning community.

---

## Team Identity Gaps

### Missing Team Customization
- **Current**: Teams have only name and basic fields
- **Required**: Logos, colors, themes, mottos, badges
- **Impact**: Teams feel generic, no tribal identity
- **Solution**: Team customization suite (10 hours)

### Missing Role Identity System
- **Current**: No role differentiation
- **Required**: FE/BE/QB roles with badges and expertise
- **Impact**: No specialization pride or unique value
- **Solution**: Role selection with visual indicators (6 hours)

### Missing Team Social Features
- **Current**: Static team membership
- **Required**: Team chat, bulletin board, activity feed
- **Impact**: No team cohesion or communication
- **Solution**: Team communication tools (12 hours)

---

## Community Building Gaps

### Missing Social Graph
- **Current**: Only team connections exist
- **Required**: Following, rivals, ilchon-style connections
- **Impact**: No broader social network
- **Solution**: Connection system beyond teams (8 hours)

### Missing Social Recognition
- **Current**: No peer interaction features
- **Required**: Endorsements, congratulations, reactions
- **Impact**: No positive reinforcement loop
- **Solution**: Social interaction widgets (6 hours)

### Missing Activity Visibility
- **Current**: No activity feed or updates
- **Required**: Real-time community activity stream
- **Impact**: Platform feels dead and static
- **Solution**: Activity feed with live updates (10 hours)

---

## Identity Display Gaps

### Missing Profile Badges
- **Current**: No visual identity markers
- **Required**: Team badges, role badges, achievements
- **Impact**: Can't show affiliation or accomplishments
- **Solution**: Badge display system (8 hours)

### Missing Today Counter
- **Current**: No profile view tracking
- **Required**: Cyworld-style visitor tracking
- **Impact**: No social validation metric
- **Solution**: Profile view counter (4 hours)

### Missing Social Status
- **Current**: No status messages or mottos
- **Required**: Personal expression tools
- **Impact**: No personality or voice
- **Solution**: Status/motto system (4 hours)

---

## Estimated Effort

**Total Social Identity Implementation**: ~68 hours
- Team features: 28 hours
- Community features: 24 hours  
- Display features: 16 hours
