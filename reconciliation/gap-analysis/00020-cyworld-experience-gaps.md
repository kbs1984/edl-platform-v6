## The Cyworld Magic Missing from EDL

### 1. The "Today" Counter ❌ MISSING
**Cyworld Had**: Obsessive visitor tracking displayed prominently
**EDL Needs**: Profile view counter with daily reset
**Current Gap**: No tracking, no display, no addiction loop
**Impact**: Missing the #1 engagement driver from Cyworld
**Implementation**: 4 hours for basic system

### 2. Minihompy Customization ❌ MISSING
**Cyworld Had**: Endless customization options for personal space
**EDL Needs**: Dashboard themes, colors, layouts, backgrounds
**Current Gap**: Zero customization, generic experience
**Impact**: No ownership feeling, no personal investment
**Implementation**: 8 hours for basic customization

### 3. Dotori → emCoin Economy ❌ MISSING
**Cyworld Had**: Virtual currency for purchases and gifts
**EDL Needs**: emCoin system with earning and spending
**Current Gap**: No economy, no transactions, no goals
**Impact**: Missing economic engagement layer
**Implementation**: 8 hours for basic economy

### 4. Ilchon Social Connections ⚠️ PARTIAL
**Cyworld Had**: Friend networks with degrees of closeness
**EDL Needs**: Team connections, supervisors, rivals
**Current Gap**: Only basic team membership exists
**Impact**: Weak social fabric, no network effects
**Implementation**: 10 hours for connection system

### 5. BGM/Victory Themes ❌ MISSING
**Cyworld Had**: Personal background music selection
**EDL Needs**: Victory themes, achievement sounds
**Current Gap**: Silent, no audio personality
**Impact**: Less emotional connection to achievements
**Implementation**: 6 hours for audio system

### 6. Photo Albums → Achievement Gallery ❌ MISSING
**Cyworld Had**: Visual displays of memories
**EDL Needs**: Achievement badges, milestone displays
**Current Gap**: No visual progress representation
**Impact**: Can't showcase journey or accomplishments
**Implementation**: 12 hours for gallery system

### 7. Diary → Activity History ❌ MISSING
**Cyworld Had**: Personal diary and updates
**EDL Needs**: Activity log, progress journal
**Current Gap**: No history, no journey documentation
**Impact**: Can't reflect on growth over time
**Implementation**: 8 hours for history system

### 8. Mini-Room → Debate Chamber ❌ MISSING
**Cyworld Had**: 3D customizable room
**EDL Needs**: Personal debate/study space
**Current Gap**: No personal venue concept
**Impact**: Missing spatial identity element
**Implementation**: 16 hours for chamber system
